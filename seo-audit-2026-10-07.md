## 4. Content quality assessment

**Strengths**

- Evidence-first policy is genuinely differentiated: comparison pages refuse unsupported rankings and link original leaderboards with exact model versions and retrieval timestamps. This matches what AI engines cite (statistics +37%, citations +40% per GEO research) and protects against Helpful-Content downgrades.
- Answer-block extractability: each guide leads with a 40-60-word "Selection framework" verdict block, uses tables for benchmark data, numbered lists for workflows, and FAQ blocks — the exact patterns Perplexity/ChatGPT extract.
- Bilingual EN/ZH clusters with full translation (not boilerplate-only) and reciprocal hreflang.
- The pricing guides honestly avoid stale price tables (method + official links), consistent with the site's authority strategy.

**Gaps (long-term, not blockers)**

1. No author personas with credentials — Organization is the only author. Acceptable for a tool site; a named engineering author would strengthen E-E-A-T for guides.
2. Original research asset absent: the 11-model same-prompt worksheet is a method, not published data. A quarterly "same-prompt shootout" dataset with dates would become the site's citable moat.
3. Thin noindexed tail (55 pages) is dormant. Either upgrade 2-3 with unique evidence to regain index coverage or keep as-is (current choice is defensible).
