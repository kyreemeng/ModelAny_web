# Pricing — ModelAny

ModelAny is a free, open-source Chrome and Microsoft Edge extension for comparing AI chatbots side by side. There is no paid tier, subscription, credit system, or in-app purchase.

## Free

- Price: $0 forever
- Limits: no ModelAny-side limits
- Includes: one prompt to up to 11 AI sites at once, side-by-side answer comparison, chat export to Markdown/Word/PDF, video and page summarization, local memory library
- Where to get it: [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/modelany/kbpnggjenonafpcigahfaeiooojepfjn) · [Microsoft Edge Add-ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/lfeckjibcfbjfdlepidpmnalpfimhdli)

## What you pay for instead

ModelAny uses the AI accounts you already have. Answers come from the official AI websites under their own plans:

- ChatGPT: OpenAI free tier or ChatGPT Plus/Pro — see openai.com/chatgpt/pricing
- Claude: Anthropic free tier or Claude Pro/Max — see anthropic.com pricing
- Gemini: Google free tier or Google AI plans — see gemini.google.com
- DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen, Doubao, GLM, Tencent Yuanbao, Wenxiaoyan: each provider's own free or paid terms

ModelAny never proxies prompts through its own servers and never resells AI access. API-cost planning guides (for people building with provider APIs instead of the chat sites) live at https://www.modelany.app/pricing/ .
