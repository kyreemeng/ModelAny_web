Hit your ChatGPT usage limit? Four ways to keep working

ChatGPT says you hit the limit—what actually works

When ChatGPT shows the usage-limit message, the cap applies to that account, on that plan, for that window. There is no safe trick that removes the cap itself; what you can do is choose how to spend the remaining quota and how to keep the task moving elsewhere until the reset.

One of those options is built into ModelAny: carry the current conversation into Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek or another supported AI with its context, so you keep working instead of re-explaining the task from scratch.

Four legitimate ways to keep working

  1. Wait for the reset: limits refresh on a rolling window. Note the time the message appeared; the same task usually runs again without burning anything extra
  2. Switch models inside ChatGPT: the fastest models generally have the most headroom—move the remaining subtasks (drafting, reformatting) to the lighter model and save the heavy model for the steps that need it
  3. Continue in another AI: on the chat page, use ModelAny → Continue in another AI to move the recent conversation into Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek or Kimi with context included, then keep going there
  4. Upgrade only if the pattern repeats: if you hit the cap several days in a row at the same hour, the honest fix is a higher plan on that provider—not a rotation of evasions

Moving the chat without losing it

Continue in another AI packs the conversation turns visible on the page into a context block and opens the destination site with it filled in. Long threads keep the most recent turns. Restate any early constraint that still matters (goal, format, deadline) at the top of your next message.

What to avoid

Bulk account creation, VPN rotation to dodge regional rules, or automation that fakes human pace violate the provider’s terms and put your account at risk. None of them raise the cap anyway. ModelAny does not do these things: it opens sites, fills your prompt and clicks send only when you tell it to.

Frequently asked questions

When does the ChatGPT usage limit reset?

Message caps refresh on a rolling window that depends on your plan and model. OpenAI documents the current limits on its help pages—the message itself usually names when you can continue.

Will ModelAny bypass the limit?

No. ModelAny does not bypass usage limits, captchas or security checks on any site. It performs the same steps you would do by hand: open the page, paste the prompt, send.

Does the other AI see my whole ChatGPT history?

No. Only the context package prepared from the current page is sent to the site you move to, with recent-turn trimming on long chats.
